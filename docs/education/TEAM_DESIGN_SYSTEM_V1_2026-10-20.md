# Team Design System v1 focused pass — 2026-10-20

## Scope and safety

This work owns `views/TeamView.tsx`, the new Team-only `views/team-operations/team-design-system-v1.css` and the isolated Team preview fixture. The earlier `education-team-operations-v1.css` also serves Staff Absence and Media and is unchanged. The actual Team component remains wired to its original AppContext/AuthContext and Firestore handlers. The preview replaces those boundaries with in-memory school records and a real `ConfirmProvider`. It cannot reach Firebase or production records. No auth policy, stored schema, rules, indexes, API, Marketing or shared Education foundation changed.

The Team job is to let school staff find a colleague, understand access, assign work, review project progress and keep a short operational conversation. The Design System v1 treatment uses existing Education tabs, buttons, fields, status pills, notices and states. The Team stylesheet now contains only scoped layout rules and canonical tokens. The mobile task board stacks its lanes rather than forcing horizontal page scrolling. Task due dates are now available in create/edit and visible on cards. Search was added to tasks and projects. Staff, task and project forms show pending, validation, cancel and failure states.
