## Fixture routes

- Base: `http://127.0.0.1:5264/`.
- Dark: `?theme=dark`.
- Empty/loading/error/restricted/no-org: `?scenario=<value>`.
- Save rejection or delayed save: `?save=error` or `?save=pending`.
- Clipboard rejection: `?clipboard=error` (provided, not exercised in this pass).

The fixture emits in-memory events only. There are no external provider requests, Firestore writes or retained production data in these checks.
